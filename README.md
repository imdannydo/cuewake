# Cuewake

**Leave it here. Find it when you’re back.**

Cuewake is a Mac app that brings your notes, checklists, links, and files back when you return to the app or page where you left them.

[Get Cuewake for Mac](https://cuewake.app/)

![Cuewake for Mac website preview](cuewake.png)

## Features

### Leave cues where you’ll need them

- 📍 **Contextual cues.** Save a note, checklist, link, or file with the app or webpage you’re using. Cuewake brings it back when you return. Open the original webpage from a saved note, or leave a cue for a specific Framer project so it comes back with that project.
- ✨ **Leave It There.** Type or dictate something like “When I open YouTube, upload the rough cut” to leave a cue for another place without opening it first. Target an app, website, folder, or file by name. Cuewake shows the destination icon as you type and tells you if it can’t find a match or finds more than one.

### Capture from the notch

- ⚡ **Quick capture.** Open the composer from the notch to jot down a thought. If you’ve copied a URL, Cuewake can open directly in Link mode. Drafts stay put when you switch between cue types.
- 💡 **Capture hints.** Get an optional prompt to leave a cue after you leave a webpage you spent time on. Adjust the timing and frequency in Settings.
- ⌨️ **Typing suggestions.** Cuewake learns phrases you use often from saved cues on your Mac. Press Tab to accept a suggestion.
- ✅ **Reusable checklists.** Paste multiple lines to create a checklist, skip or include individual steps, and reuse a checklist from the Dashboard or notch.
- 📎 **File Shelf.** Drop files into the notch to hold them temporarily, then drag them into another app. You can also attach files to a cue and repair a missing file reference if the original moves.

### Find cues and follow up

- 🗂️ **Dashboard.** Browse all cues or narrow them down by type or status, including Waiting, Later, and Done. Edit or copy saved notes, defer cues, and adjust the text size on Dashboard cards.
- 🔎 **Command search.** Press ⌘K to find a cue or run an action from the keyboard. You can open a result directly or show all matching cues in the Dashboard.
- ⏰ **Timed reminders.** Write a time in plain language, such as “tomorrow at 9” or “in 2 hours.” Snooze reminders or review what’s due today, upcoming, or overdue.

### Keep your cues on your Mac

- 💾 **Local storage and backup.** Cues are stored locally. Export them as Markdown or back them up and restore missing cues. Backups retain references to attached files. The original files stay in their own locations.
- ↩️ **Undo and recovery.** Undo a deletion with ⌘Z or recover cues from Recently Deleted for up to 30 days.
- 🔒 **Folder access controls.** Manage access in Settings. When Cuewake looks for a folder or file in Desktop, Documents, or Downloads, it asks for access and uses the names and locations to find a match.
- 🌍 **Multiple languages.** Use Cuewake in English, Arabic, Chinese, French, German, Japanese, or Spanish. Arabic supports a right-to-left layout.

## Requirements

Cuewake requires **macOS 14 or later** and an **Apple silicon Mac**. It’s available as a one-time purchase.
